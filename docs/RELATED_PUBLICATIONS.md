# Related electromagnetic and pipeline-inspection publications

[中文说明](#中文说明) · [BibTeX](related_publications.bib) · [Product materials](product_materials/README.md)

These peer-reviewed papers provide the research lineage around compact electromagnetic sensing, eddy-current in-line inspection, magnetic coupling, multisensor processing and physics/digital AI. They are linked here as related technical context. A paper is **not** presented as direct validation of the P110 or 406-mm stress datasets unless a case-study record explicitly establishes that connection.

## Publications

[DOI: 10.1109/TII.2025.3648328](https://doi.org/10.1109/TII.2025.3648328) · [UESTC author record](https://faculty.uestc.edu.cn/gaobin/zh_CN/lwcg/153392/content/79751.htm)

   Relevance: compact LC-driven sensing and high-resolution pipeline-inspection information recovery.

2. **Rui Chen, Bin Gao, Guixin Qin, Chao Chang, Yukuan Kang, Hongjiang Ren, Diyuan Zou, Qiuping Ma, and Wai Lok Woo.** “Physical and Digital Dual-Driven AI Framework for Enhanced Electromagnetic Perception of Nondestructive Testing Tomography.” *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics*, 21(11), 8858–8869, 2025. [DOI: 10.1109/TII.2025.3588620](https://doi.org/10.1109/TII.2025.3588620) · [UESTC author record](https://faculty.uestc.edu.cn/gaobin/zh_CN/lwcg/153392/content/79263.htm)

3. **Qin Tang, Bin Gao, Qiuping Ma, Gaige Ru, Songwen Xue, Wenze Shi, Fei Luo, and Wai Lok Woo.** “Physical Coupling Fusion of Electromagnetic Acoustic Guided Wave With ‘Near-Zero’ Magnetic Sensing System for Multitype Defect Detection.” *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement*, 74, Article 3526511, 1–11, 2025. [DOI: 10.1109/TIM.2025.3555696](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2025.3555696) · [UESTC author record](https://faculty.uestc.edu.cn/gaobin/zh_CN/lwcg/153392/content/15967.htm)

4. **Gaige Ru, Bin Gao, Songwen Xue, Jun Xian, Yuxi Xie, and Wai Lok Woo.** “Pipeline Integrity Gauges Based on Dynamic Magnetic Coupling Sensing Technology.” *NDT & E International*, 151, Article 103307, 2025.
