Two experiments, two measurement contracts

The projects are displayed at equal level but are never merged semantically. Each keeps its own sensors, calibration truth and claim boundary.

P110 magnetic + strain

P110 casing experiment

  • 10.54 m casing, 139.70 mm outside diameter, 9.17 mm wall.
  • Three-axis magnetic output and matched strain-gauge references.
  • No independent remanence stream and no ETP modality.
Dataset DOI Case study
406 MEM + remanence + ETP

406-mm multimodal experiment

  • Four-point-bending calibration plus repeated blind pull cycles.
  • Shared MEM/remanence magnetic array and independent complex ETP.
  • Strain-gauge reference in calibration; no blind-cycle MPa truth.
Dataset DOI Case study
Engineering boundary. Current evidence supports controlled same-pipe relative-change and repeatability research. Direct transferable MPa output remains disabled until frozen calibration, uncertainty analysis and independent-pipe blind validation are completed.
